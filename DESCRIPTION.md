# OmniStock AI: Agentic Supply Chain Intelligence

**OmniStock AI** is an advanced, multi-agent AI platform designed to eliminate stockouts and reduce overstocking for FMCG and biscuit distributors in India. By transitioning from traditional *predictive AI* to an **Agentic AI** framework, the system doesn't just forecast demand—it autonomously takes action.

### Key Highlights:
- **Autonomous Ordering:** Generates weekly Purchase Orders (POs) automatically using a LangGraph multi-agent orchestration pattern with human-in-the-loop oversight.
- **Festive Intelligence:** Integrates local festive calendars and weather signals using an XGBoost + Prophet ensemble to anticipate massive demand spikes (e.g., Diwali, Durga Puja).
- **Hyper-Local Promotions:** Leverages LLMs to instantly design and broadcast SMS/WhatsApp-ready promotional bundles in regional languages to clear slow-moving inventory.
- **Q-Commerce Ready:** Integrates real-time demand signals from dark stores to calculate emergency replenishments in under 5 minutes.
- **Data Pipeline:** Powered by a robust Bronze-Silver-Gold Medallion architecture for real-time analytics and predictive ML processing.

OmniStock AI tackles the ₹15,000 Crore "Visibility Gap" problem in the Indian FMCG sector, driving a projected 15-30% reduction in stockouts and saving distributors lakhs of rupees in expired inventory.
